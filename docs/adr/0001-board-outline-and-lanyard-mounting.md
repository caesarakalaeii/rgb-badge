# ADR 0001: Board outline and lanyard mounting

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-10-17

## Context

The matrix PCB hangs from a lanyard. Earlier iterations used small 10×12 mm
corner tabs with bare-FR4 holes, which were too fragile for a ~300 € board. Two
tab redesigns (tabs sticking up from the top strip, H-shaped side tabs) kept the
stress-concentrating tab necks.

J2 (power, JST VH B10PS-VH, horizontal, on B.Cu) sat flush in the top-right
corner with its cable exiting through the top edge. That left no room for a
right-hand hole inside the 71 mm board width, and any board material above J2
would block the plug.

## Decision

- The outline is a plain rounded rectangle: x 1.3..72.36 mm, y -19.7..130.4 mm
  (71.06 × 150.1 mm), 5 mm corner radius. No tabs.
- A 16 mm band added above the old top strip holds two lanyard holes and J2.
- Lanyard holes (H1, H2): 5 mm plated, 11 mm GND ring on all copper layers,
  12 GND stitch pads, centered 6.5 mm from the top and side edges (4 mm FR4 web
  from the drill to each edge). Board-only footprints, no schematic symbol.
- J2 stays horizontal with the cable exiting the top edge, centered between the
  holes. J1 and the lane buffers are unchanged.
- Generated by `cad/redesign_top_band.py`, checked by `cad/test_board.py`.

## Alternatives considered

- Widen the board to ~91 mm and keep the old hole positions: no parts move, but
  ~25 % more 6-layer area.
- Turn J2 to exit the right edge: J2 is 40.6 mm long, so the board would grow
  to ~71 × 170 mm.
- Swap J2 for the vertical B10P-VH (cable plugs into the back): the board grows
  less (~145 mm), but U1/TVS1 must move and the cable sticks out of the back.

## Consequences

- The board grows from 134 to 150 mm tall; width is unchanged.
- The lanyard and the power cable both leave through the top edge.
- The J2-to-U1 power entry had to be re-laid (see ADR 0002).
